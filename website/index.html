What this project is designed to demonstrate+

Storage & permissions

Store the site in S3, keep the bucket private and allow the intended CloudFront distribution to read its files.

Delivery & HTTPS

Serve the portfolio through CloudFront and use its HTTPS address without buying a custom domain.

Deployment & updates

Upload a small set of static files, understand caching and test the site after each update.
